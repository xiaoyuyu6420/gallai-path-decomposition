exactly one of five strictly separated strengths, mirroring the claim-strength
declaration binding the rest of the paper:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \tierThm{} --- fully proved on paper and independently audited;

review passed), not promoted to theorem;

over the full explicit domain (instance-by-instance, zero counterexamples),
independently audited;

account;

a proof).

a \texttt{won} verdict is not a theorem; candidate-, machine-check- and
profile-level items are never written as proved; every external citation
carries its verification level.
